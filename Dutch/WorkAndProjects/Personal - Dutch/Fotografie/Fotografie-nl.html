ABOUT THIS WORK

From raw capture to finished image

Photography editing is as much a craft as the shoot itself. Each image below started as a raw capture and was transformed through careful retouching — adjusting light, shadow, colour, skin tones, and composition until the photo matched the original vision.

Drag the vertical slider on each image to reveal the before on the left and the finished edit on the right. Notice how colour grading, exposure correction, and targeted retouching bring each photo to life.
