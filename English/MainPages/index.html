Behind the lens

I’m Stijn, a 25-year-old video editor and creative storyteller from the Netherlands. My favourite part of the process is the editing, turning raw footage into something beautiful, funny or awesome. I work primarily in DaVinci Resolve, but I always experiment with other softwares, such as the Adobe Creative Suite. Beyond editing, I like to fly my DJI Mini 3 around and shoot pictures and videos with my Sony A7SII to keep the creative spark alive
