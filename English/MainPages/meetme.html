The person behind the lens

Hi, I’m Stijn, a 25-year-old video editor and creative storyteller based in the Netherlands. I finished an Associate Degree in Digital Technology & Media at the Hogeschool Utrecht in Amersfoort, and I’ve been building up my editing and videography skills through my internships.

My passion for video editing started when I began experimenting with making videos for fun when I was younger. That interest reignited again when I wanted to start a (very unprofessional) YouTube channel a few years ago. That experience taught me the basics of editing, and from that moment, I knew I wanted to do this for a living.

“I don’t like a job where I do the same thing over and over again, I want to be creative for a living”

My toolkit

I work primarily in DaVinci Resolve (with my own studio license) and I’ve been expanding my skills to other softwares like Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects and Photoshop.
I always love creating new things, even if it’s an effect or trick I’ve never tried before, I always want to push myself to the limits to learn that new thing. Slowly I’ve also dipped my toes into colour grading and motion graphics.
When I'm not editing, I'm usually reading, 3D printing, gaming, or finding new ways to experiment with video. These hobbies keep my creativity fresh and remind me why I love this work in the first place.
