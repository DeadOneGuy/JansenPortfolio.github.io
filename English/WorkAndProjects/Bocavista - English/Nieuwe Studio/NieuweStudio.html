ABOUT THIS PROJECT

New setting in the spotlight

For the studio where I did my internship, they had created a new set for the various shoots that take place there. To attract more clients and showcase what the studio has to offer, we recorded a video to present this new set.

The goal of the video was to give a good impression of the new set and the possibilities of the studio. The video should show how the space can be used for different types of productions and at the same time give a good impression of the studio's atmosphere. During the recording I operated the autocue. After that I did the complete video edit, including the audio mix and colour grade.

My tasks:
- Operating the autocue during the recording
- Creating the complete edit
- Audio mix
- Colour grade
