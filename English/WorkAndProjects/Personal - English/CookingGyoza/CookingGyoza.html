About this work

A video style expriment: cozy cooking video

One of my hobby videos. I wanted to experiment more with different video styles, so for this video I chose one of the dishes I was going to cook that evening and decided to make a cozy cooking video.

The video was shot with the intention of using a voice-over, but I ended up liking the music and overall vibe so much that I decided to keep it simple and use creative titles integrated into the objects in the video instead. I also experimented with colour grading in DaVinci Resolve to give the video a vintage look.
